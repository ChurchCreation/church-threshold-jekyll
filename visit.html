I'm new

What you are walking into

Written for the person sitting in the parking lot deciding whether to come in.

Straight answers

The things people actually ask

How long is it?
Seventy-five minutes, and we finish when we say we will. Doors open thirty minutes early and there is decent coffee, free.
What do people wear?
Whatever they were already wearing. This is Austin.
Will anyone make me stand up or say my name?
No. We do not do that, ever. You can sit at the back, leave in the last song, and come back six times before anyone learns your name.
What happens with my kids?
Threshold Kids runs at every service for birth through fifth grade. You check them in, you get a code, nobody else can collect them. Every volunteer is background-checked and there are two adults in every room, always.
Is there an offering I have to take part in?
There is an offering. Guests should ignore it entirely — we say so out loud most weeks. Nothing is passed down the row.
What if I am not sure what I believe?
Then you are in good company, including on the staff. Nobody is going to corner you about it.
Is the building accessible?
Step-free throughout, accessible restrooms, reserved parking by the main door, and a hearing loop. Ask a host in a grey shirt for anything.
Start to finish

How the hour and a quarter goes

  1. 01

    You park and someone says hello

    Grey shirts are hosts. They will point you at coffee and kids check-in and then leave you alone.

  2. 02

    Four songs

    Loud. Some people sing, some stand with their arms folded, and both are completely normal here.

  3. 03

    Thirty-five minutes of teaching

    Out of the Bible, in plain English, with the hard bits left in rather than skipped.

  4. 04

    Response and close

    A song, a prayer, an offering guests should ignore, and out.

  5. 05

    Coffee, if you want it

    Plenty of people leave straight away. That is genuinely fine.
