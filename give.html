Other ways

Not everything has to be an app

Bank transfer
Cheapest for us — no processing fee at all. Details are on the back of the connection card, or email the office.
In person
There are boxes by the doors at both campuses. Cash and cheques both fine.
Stock and donor-advised funds
Both work and both are more tax-efficient than you would expect. The office will walk you through it.
Giving to something specific
The food pantry and the building fund both take designated gifts. Put it in the reference and it goes there.
